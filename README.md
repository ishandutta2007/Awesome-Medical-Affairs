# Awesome-Medical-Affairs

## Top Medical Affairs Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Medical Science Liaison (MSL) Engagement, KOL Management, Medical Content, Scientific Exchange, Insights & Compliant HCP Interactions*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Medical Affairs**. These systems support pharmaceutical and life-sciences Medical Affairs teams in managing KOL/HCP engagement, MSL field activities, medical information, scientific content, congress insights, and compliant interactions separate from commercial promotion.



**Examples** include Aktana, Veeva Medical CRM, IQVIA OCE, Indegene, Within3, Medable Medical Affairs, Agnitio, TrueBlue, Medscape Consult, and related Medical Affairs / MSL platforms (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Purpose-built Medical Affairs platforms with pharma-grade compliance, KOL management, medical information workflows, and field-medical CRM capabilities are almost exclusively commercial. Open-source activity is limited to emerging AI/agent frameworks for pharma processes, research prototypes for scientific intelligence, and general CRM or content tools that can be adapted. This section lists every relevant project and realistic building block found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Veeva Medical CRM / Vault Medical](https://www.veeva.com/)**  

  Industry-standard life-sciences CRM and content platform with dedicated Medical Affairs capabilities for MSL engagement, medical content, and compliant HCP interactions, often integrated with the broader Veeva commercial stack.



- **[IQVIA OCE (Orchestrated Customer Engagement)](https://www.iqvia.com/)**  

  Life-sciences customer engagement platform with strong data assets, supporting medical and commercial teams in HCP orchestration, insights, and next-best-action style workflows.



- **[Aktana](https://www.aktana.com/)**  

  AI-driven next-best-action and orchestration platform that connects brand strategy to field and digital execution, frequently used alongside CRM systems for Medical and Commercial teams.



- **[Indegene, Within3, Agnitio, TrueBlue](https://www.indegene.com/)**  

  Solutions covering medical content, virtual scientific engagement, KOL collaboration communities, and Medical Affairs technology services.



- **[Medable Medical Affairs & related platforms](https://www.medable.com/)**  

  Technology supporting clinical and medical workflows, including aspects of Medical Affairs engagement and evidence generation.



- **[Medscape Consult & professional medical networks](https://www.medscape.com/)**  

  Platforms facilitating peer-to-peer and HCP scientific discussion that Medical Affairs teams monitor or engage with.



- **[Other commercial Medical Affairs & MSL platforms](https://www.veeva.com/)**  

  Additional tools for KOL management, medical information request (MIR) handling, congress insights, and field-medical CRM.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Mediforce](https://github.com/appsilon/mediforce)**  

  Open-source platform for orchestrating AI agents and human workflows in pharma, designed with compliance, auditability, and GxP-oriented oversight in mind. Can support process automation relevant to Medical Affairs and other regulated functions.



- **[DS4MedAffairs](https://github.com/ds4cabs/DS4MedAffairs)**  

  Project focused on AI capabilities for Medical Affairs—publication intelligence, congress summarization, MSL copilots, scientific response drafting, and KOL-related workflows.



- **[Pharma / life-sciences AI agent prototypes](https://github.com/search?q=pharma+OR+medical+affairs+OR+MSL+OR+KOL+AI)**  

  Emerging multi-agent and LLM-based tools for competitive intelligence, publication monitoring, KOL mapping, and scientific content assistance.



- **[General open CRM frameworks (adaptable)](https://github.com/search?q=CRM+open+source)**  

  Open CRM systems that some organizations customize for basic HCP/KOL interaction tracking (require significant compliance and validation work for regulated use).



- **[Content & document management open tools](https://github.com/search?q=document+management+OR+content+management+open+source)**  

  Self-hosted content platforms that can support medical content repositories with appropriate access controls and audit trails.



- **[Publication & literature intelligence helpers](https://github.com/search?q=PubMed+OR+publication+tracking+OR+literature+review)**  

  Open tools for monitoring scientific literature and congress outputs that feed Medical Affairs insights processes.



- **[Workflow & approval engines](https://github.com/search?q=approval+workflow+OR+GxP+workflow)**  

  Open workflow systems that can implement medical review and approval chains when properly validated.



- **[Smaller pharma-rep / medical CRM demos](https://github.com/search?q=pharma+CRM+OR+medical+device+CRM)**  

  Educational or prototype CRM applications oriented toward pharma or medical-device field teams.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **AI/agent orchestration for regulated processes**: Mediforce and similar frameworks for transparent, auditable human–AI workflows.

- **Scientific intelligence prototypes**: DS4MedAffairs-style tools for publications, congresses, and MSL support.

- **Composable stacks**: Open CRM or content core + literature APIs + workflow engine + strict audit logging for limited internal use cases.

- **Data foundations**: Open literature and trials databases as inputs to custom insight tools.

- Full Medical Affairs CRM, KOL platforms, and compliant content management at enterprise pharma scale remain commercial.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

There is no mature, production-ready open-source equivalent to Veeva Medical CRM, IQVIA OCE, Aktana, or specialized Medical Affairs platforms.  

Emerging open projects such as **Mediforce** (agent/human workflow orchestration) and **DS4MedAffairs**-style AI tools provide building blocks for scientific intelligence and process automation.  

General open CRM, content, and workflow systems can be adapted only with rigorous validation, access control, and compliance design.  

Commercial platforms dominate because of deep life-sciences data models, regulatory alignment, audit trails, integration with commercial CRM and medical information systems, and vendor support required by global pharma. Most Medical Affairs organizations rely on commercial solutions; open-source is best suited for research, prototypes, or narrowly scoped internal tools under strict governance.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Medical Affairs activities are highly regulated. Systems handling HCP interactions, medical information, or scientific content must support applicable pharmaceutical regulations, privacy rules, and company compliance policies. Incorrect configuration or lack of auditability can create significant regulatory risk.

- Open-source tools offer transparency but generally lack the validated compliance features, life-sciences data models, and support of commercial Medical Affairs platforms. Any custom or open-source solution intended for regulated use requires thorough validation, security review, and legal/compliance approval before deployment.
